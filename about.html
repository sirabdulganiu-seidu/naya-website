WHO WE ARE

About Nanumba Youth Association

The Nanumba Youth Association (NAYA) is a non-partisan, non-profit and non-governmental youth association committed to promoting the development and unity of purpose among the Nanumba people.

NAYA seeks to bring Nanumba youth together, promote education and integrity, and support initiatives that contribute to the sustainable development of the Nanung community.

The Association provides a platform through which young people can come together, identify challenges affecting their communities, advocate for positive change, and participate meaningfully in community and national development.

01

Our Vision

To build a united, educated, and economically empowered Nanumba youth community contributing meaningfully to national development.

02

Our Mission

To mobilize, educate, and empower Nanumba youth through education support, community development initiatives, and leadership development.

03

Our Purpose

To promote unity, education, integrity and sustainable development among Nanung, while encouraging young people to participate actively in the development of their communities.

MEMBERSHIP

Who Can Become a Member?

Membership of the Nanumba Youth Association is open to persons of Nanumba descent and persons residing within the Nanung community who subscribe to the objectives and principles of the Association.

Members are expected to uphold the values and objectives of NAYA and contribute positively to the development, unity and wellbeing of the Nanumba community.

01

Education

Supporting education and encouraging the development of knowledge and skills among Nanumba youth.

02

Community Development

Supporting initiatives and advocacy efforts aimed at improving the social and economic development of Nanung communities.

03

Youth Empowerment

Mobilizing and empowering young people while promoting leadership, responsibility, integrity and meaningful participation in development.
