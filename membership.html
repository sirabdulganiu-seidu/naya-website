MEMBERSHIP

Become Part of NAYA

Join a community committed to unity, education, integrity, youth empowerment and the sustainable development of Nanung.

JOIN NAYA

Your Participation Matters

The Nanumba Youth Association welcomes eligible persons who identify with the objectives and values of the Association and are willing to contribute positively to the development and unity of the Nanumba people.

Membership provides an opportunity for individuals to participate in the affairs of the Association, contribute ideas, support community initiatives and work with other members towards common development goals.

01

Eligibility

Membership is open to persons of Nanumba descent and other persons who meet the membership requirements of the Association and support its objectives.

02

Participation

Members are encouraged to participate actively in the programmes, meetings, activities and development initiatives of NAYA.

03

Responsibility

Members are expected to uphold the values, objectives and principles of the Association and contribute positively to its work.

WHY JOIN NAYA?

Be Part of Something Bigger

NAYA provides a platform for young people and community-minded individuals to work together, share ideas and contribute to the development of Nanung.

01

Community

Connect with other people who are committed to the progress and wellbeing of the Nanung community.

02

Leadership

Develop opportunities for responsible leadership, collaboration, service and meaningful participation.

03

Impact

Contribute your skills, ideas and resources to initiatives that seek to create positive change in Nanung.

MEMBERSHIP VALUES

What We Stand For

Members of NAYA are expected to support the principles of unity, integrity, accountability, respect, service and constructive participation.

These values help strengthen the Association and promote cooperation among members as they work towards the development of Nanung.

HOW TO JOIN

Interested in Becoming a Member?

Prospective members should contact the leadership of the Nanumba Youth Association through the Association's official communication channels for information about the current membership process and requirements.

Membership procedures, registration arrangements and any applicable requirements will be communicated by the Association through its authorised channels.
