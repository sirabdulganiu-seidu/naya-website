OUR WORK

Projects for Community Development

The Nanumba Youth Association (NAYA) supports and undertakes initiatives that respond to the needs and aspirations of Nanumba youth and the wider Nanung community.

Our project areas are guided by the objectives of the Association and focus on education, youth empowerment, community development, advocacy, leadership and social wellbeing.

As projects are developed and implemented, this page will provide information about their objectives, beneficiaries, implementation activities, partners and outcomes.

01

Education Projects

Initiatives designed to support education, improve learning opportunities and encourage young people to pursue academic and skills development.

02

Youth Development Projects

Projects focused on developing the knowledge, skills, leadership capacity and opportunities available to Nanumba youth.

03

Community Development Projects

Community-oriented initiatives addressing identified social, economic and developmental needs within Nanung.

04

Advocacy Projects

Projects that raise awareness and advocate for issues affecting Nanumba youth and the wider community.

05

Leadership Projects

Initiatives that promote responsible leadership, integrity, civic participation and effective youth engagement.

06

Social Development Projects

Initiatives that promote unity, social cohesion and the general wellbeing of individuals and communities within Nanung.
