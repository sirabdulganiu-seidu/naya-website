WHO WE ARE

Building a stronger future for the Nanumba people.

The Nanumba Youth Association (NAYA) is a non-partisan, non-profit and non-governmental organization committed to promoting unity, education, integrity and sustainable development among the people of Nanung. The Association seeks to mobilize, educate and empower Nanumba youth through education support, community development initiatives and leadership development.
