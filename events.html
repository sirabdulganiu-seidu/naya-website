OUR EVENTS

Connecting People Through Engagement

Events provide an important platform for the Nanumba Youth Association to bring young people, community members, stakeholders and partners together around issues affecting Nanung.

NAYA events may include community engagements, youth forums, educational activities, advocacy programmes, meetings, leadership activities and other initiatives that support the objectives of the Association.

Details of confirmed events will be published on this page, including the date, venue, purpose and relevant participation information.
