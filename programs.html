Our Programs

Initiatives focused on empowering Nanumba youth, promoting education, strengthening unity and contributing to sustainable community development.

WHAT WE DO

Building a Stronger Nanung Through Youth Action

The Nanumba Youth Association (NAYA) undertakes programmes, advocacy initiatives and community activities designed to promote the development and unity of purpose of the Nanumba people.

Our programmes focus on areas that directly affect the wellbeing and future of young people, including education, leadership, community development, youth empowerment and advocacy.

Through collaboration with communities, institutions, development partners and other stakeholders, NAYA seeks to create opportunities for young people to contribute positively to the development of Nanung and Ghana.

01

Education Support

Promoting access to quality education and supporting initiatives that improve educational opportunities and outcomes for young people within the Nanung community.

02

Youth Empowerment

Creating opportunities for young people to develop their skills, knowledge, leadership capacity and ability to participate meaningfully in community development.

03

Community Development

Supporting initiatives and community-based activities intended to improve the social and economic wellbeing of the Nanung community.

04

Advocacy & Awareness

Advocating for issues affecting Nanumba youth and the wider community while creating awareness around matters important to sustainable development and social wellbeing.

05

Unity & Social Cohesion

Promoting unity, cooperation and a shared sense of purpose among Nanumba youth and communities while encouraging peaceful and constructive engagement.

06

Leadership Development

Encouraging responsible leadership, integrity and active civic participation among young people to prepare them to contribute effectively to the future of their communities.

OUR APPROACH

Working Together for Impact

NAYA believes that sustainable development requires the participation of young people, traditional authorities, communities, civil society organisations, government institutions and other stakeholders.

We therefore seek to build partnerships and create platforms through which ideas, resources and expertise can be brought together to address challenges affecting Nanung.

01

Identify

Identify challenges and opportunities affecting Nanumba youth and communities.

02

Collaborate

Work with relevant stakeholders to develop practical responses and initiatives.

03

Act & Advocate

Implement initiatives and advocate for positive and sustainable change.

GET INVOLVED

Be Part of the Change

The development of Nanung requires the commitment and participation of its people, especially its youth. NAYA welcomes constructive ideas, partnerships and participation that advance the objectives of the Association.
