LEADERSHIP OF NAYA

Serving with Purpose and Integrity

The Nanumba Youth Association (NAYA) is guided by an elected leadership structure responsible for providing direction, coordinating the activities of the Association, representing its interests and ensuring that its objectives are pursued in accordance with its constitution.

The leadership is expected to promote unity, accountability, transparency, integrity and active participation among members while working towards the social, educational and economic development of the Nanung community.

President

[Vice President's Full Name]

Vice President

[General Secretary's Full Name]

General Secretary

The General Secretary is responsible for the administrative and secretarial affairs of the Association, including maintaining official records and supporting effective communication.

[Organizer's Full Name]

Organizer

The Organizer coordinates programmes, activities and mobilisation efforts of the Association and supports participation by members.

[Treasurer's Full Name]

Treasurer

The Treasurer is responsible for supporting the proper management, recording and accountability of the financial affairs of the Association in accordance with its constitution.

[Public Relations Officer's Full Name]

Public Relations Officer

The Public Relations Officer supports communication, public information and the promotion of the activities and objectives of NAYA.

OUR COMMITMENT

Leadership Principles

NAYA expects its leaders to serve the interests of the Association and its members with integrity, accountability, transparency and commitment.

The leadership shall work collaboratively with members, stakeholders and communities to advance the objectives of the Association and contribute to the development of Nanung.
